02 / THE PEOPLE BEHIND DIELATWO FRIENDS. ONE SHARED QUESTION.

We had the products.
We still had questions.

↳

We’re best friends who discovered that “Have you tried this cream?” could comfortably fill an entire evening. One of us would investigate the ingredients and the research behind them, while the other wanted to know who the product was designed for and whether it delivered on its promises. The cream had, unknowingly, entered a fairly thorough interview process.

Yet we still stood in front of our own skincare shelves wondering what to use together and what our skin actually needed. With backgrounds in product, engineering and AI, we eventually started building the thing we kept wishing existed: Diela.

We’re building it around work and family, debating details we thought we’d agreed on, and discovering that some excellent ideas become considerably less excellent when you try them. We want Diela to earn a place in your everyday life, and we’d love your help figuring out what would make it useful in yours.

Who is Diela for?

People who use skincare but feel unsure about their routine or what to change. A few essentials are enough; you don’t need to buy more.

Do I need an established routine?

No. Start by adding the products you already use, then create morning and evening routines you can edit. Manual routine creation and tracking are part of the free product being developed.

How does Diela learn about my skin?

You share your skin type and concerns in your profile. Later check-ins, notes and photos record what you notice over time. Diela does not automatically detect your skin type or diagnose conditions, and these records cannot prove what caused a change.

What will AI do?

Planned AI assistance will use your products and the skin information you share to suggest a routine for you to review. You choose whether to make changes. Features using personal information with AI will ask for consent and explain data use and controls before launch.

Are these working app screens?

These are noninteractive prototype excerpts with example data, including planned AI suggestions. They do not analyse your skin or save information. The interface and features may change during development.

What happens when I join?

A short Tally form asks for your name and email so we can contact you about testing. Diela is in development, with no confirmed testing or launch date. Joining is free and does not guarantee a testing place.
