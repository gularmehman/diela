03 / BEFORE YOU JOIN

A little
more clarity.

Clear expectations.
From the beginning.

Can I use Diela yet?

Diela is in development. Join the testing list to register your interest. Testing invitations and a launch date are not confirmed yet.

Do I need to buy new products?

Your existing shelf is the starting point. Diela is being designed to help you organise your products and build a routine around what you already own.

What does the AI do?

AI-assisted routine creation is part of the planned app. It is intended to use your products and skin information to suggest a starting point you can review. It does not replace professional care.

What about my personal information?

Our testing-list form is hosted by Tally and asks for your name and email. Planned app features that use your personal information with AI will ask for consent, with data use and controls explained before those features launch.

Are these working app screens?

They are selected, noninteractive excerpts from Diela’s latest prototype, using example data. They do not analyse your skin or save information. The interface and features may change during development.

What happens when I join?

A short Tally form opens so you can share your name and email. We’ll use your details to contact you about testing. Joining is free and does not guarantee a place in the first testing group.

A note on privacy.

This website uses a Tally form to collect testing-list registrations. The form asks for your name and email; please do not submit skin details or photos. Its product excerpts use example data and do not save your interactions. This site’s code does not add advertising trackers or analytics.

Joining the testing list opens a form hosted by Tally, which processes the details you submit. We use these details to contact you about Diela testing. To leave the list or ask about your information, email hello@diela.app. Read the privacy information in the form before submitting. Hosting and access may also involve the hosting platform’s own data processing.
