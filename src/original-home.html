TWO FRIENDS. A SHARED QUESTION.

It started with
our own skin.

We loved the research. We still had questions about our own routines.

We’re two best friends. A conversation about skincare could turn into an evening of comparing ingredients, sharing papers and wondering what actually made sense for us.

Our backgrounds are product, technology and AI. Diela is where those skills meet something personal: a wish to understand our skincare in the context of our lives.

We’re still building, testing our assumptions and figuring things out. We’d love you to be part of that.
